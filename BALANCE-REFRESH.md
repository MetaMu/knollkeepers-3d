# 3D balance refresh — September 12, 2026

Imported the current 2D bonuses and recalculations without modifying the 2D source, 3D map, character files or spell styles.

- Enemy HP: original formula × 0.9 (current 2D adjustment) × 1.28 (3D difficulty adjustment), or 15.2% more than the preceding 3D release. Existing wave/chapter scaling remains.
- Spawn spacing: 10% wider, matching 2D. Venom damage against guardians: 10% higher, matching 2D.
- Chapter 1 wave 5: +500 milestone gold; wave 10: +1000. These are added to the ordinary 35 + wave × 5 reward, exactly once.
- Chapter transitions: carry remaining gold, grant +250, clear guardians/allies/projectiles and reset ability cooldowns. Recover up to eight shrine health as before.
- KnowME Deep Freeze: free, once in chapter 1 with a deployed KnowME and on-screen enemies during combat. Freezes existing on-screen enemies and their venom attacks for five simulation seconds; later spawns are not affected. Resetting the game restores it.
- Reno remains 1500 gold. Guardian prices, damage and upgrades remain unchanged.

Validation includes all 53 wave transitions, exact reward totals, no repeated milestone payout, freeze expiry/gating, finite rigs, rendering recipes, placement and map checks. Increased enemy durability is verified; this does not claim a measured player win-rate change.
