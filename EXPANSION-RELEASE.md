# Knoll Keepers 3D — three-chapter expansion

2026-09-12. This release changes only the separate 3D game, not Lost Realm's original 2D edition.

- Chapters: Forest (15 waves), Ashen (18), Eclipse Citadel (20).
- Seven guardians: Lady Never Scared unlocks in chapter 2; Phil Heal in chapter 3.
- Lady heals nearby guardians and converts non-boss mutants into a rigged allied demon. Phil uses serialized clone strikes. Reno Mo is a separate 1500-gold combat summon with a 45-second cooldown.
- Tower HP, poison damage and paid replacements. Fresh chapter boards carry ending gold, including the final wave reward, with no automatic tower refund or extra chapter bonus.
- Longer 1936-unit 3D route, fourteen knolls, original glass/copper shrine and camera controls preserved.
- Animated GIF victory scroll, falling magical stars and golden confetti.
- Four new reference-matched GLBs with conservative regional rigs, 512px runtime textures, and idle/cast clips. Demon has a stiff stop-motion walk. These are not humanoid-retargeting or finger-articulation rigs.
- Genuine 3D combat effects and crystal/citadel scenery. Existing mutant enemy sprite rendering is retained from the previous version.

Validation: 39 automated tests pass, including GLB hashes, finite geometry, normalized skin weights, animation channels, unlocks and exact costs. A deterministic progression harness traverses all 53 waves (not a claim of a human full-campaign victory). Local browser checks cover loading, tower placement, active combat, Lady conversion, Phil strikes, Reno activation/resumption, and victory-scroll continuation. Long-session difficulty tuning remains playtest-driven.

Four Hyper3D generation jobs cost 2 credits total, verified by account balance 40 -> 38. No retries, paid splitting, purchases or subscription changes. Source models, prompts, Blender files and receipts retained privately in the local workbench; named GLBs and previews also delivered to the Desktop.
